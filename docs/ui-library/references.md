# References Andrew picked (16 Nov 2026)

Watched frame by frame. What each shows, and what TechnoSys takes from it.

## 1. Smooth animations: "Smart Home Mobile App" (Cipherslab)
https://pin.it/4gf8FUnh5

What it does:
- **Cards that come alive when switched on:** a device card fills with the brand color and its icon turns white; the toggle slides with a little stretch.
- **One accent color** (violet) with very pale tints for room cards. Everything else is white and light grey.
- **Header that shrinks** as you scroll the list.
- **Screen change:** a quick fade, then the grid of room cards appears **one after another** (about half a second for the whole grid).
- Center "+" button in the tab bar.

What we take: interactive cards that fill on select, staggered card entrance, collapsing header, a single strong accent.

## 2. Button interactions: Members / Chats / Activity app
https://ph.pinterest.com/pin/874965033869981103/

What it does:
- A small status button **springs open into a short menu** (Online, Idle, Do not disturb, Invisible). Each option is a colored circle and they pop out one after another.
- Picking one **collapses the menu back into the button**, which now shows the chosen state.
- The tab bar **morphs into a floating pill** that holds context (the current group).

What we take: the expanding action button (for "New request": Leave, Time correction, Tool problem, Something else), and the button keeping the chosen state.

## 3. Screen transitions: Planner / "Management Mobile App" (UIDesignz)
https://ph.pinterest.com/pin/892275744915873282/

What it does:
- Pressing "Get started" **floods the screen with the brand color from the button** like a wave. The next screen is then revealed as the color drains away.
- The next screen's cards **slide up into place**.
- "Add new task" rises as a **sheet over a dimmed background**, with simple chips for choices.
- Hand-drawn illustration on onboarding screens.

What we take: the color-wave reveal for **one** special moment only (finishing first-time setup, see `motion.md`), cards sliding up on arrival, short choice sheets with chips.

## Not taken
- Rainbow pastel icon grids (too many colors, Andrew's feedback).
- Stock illustrations: Techy is our only illustration.
- QR attendance, payroll ("Gaji"), reimbursements, social login: out of scope.
