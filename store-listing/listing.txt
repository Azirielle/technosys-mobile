Full description (max 4000):
TechnoSys is the work app for Technocycle Corp.'s aircon installation and cleaning crews.

• Time in and out at the job site. The app checks you're at the site; if you're outside it, a selfie and a reason go to the office for review. No signal? It saves your time and sends it later.
• See today's job and your schedule: where, when, who is on your crew, and directions.
• Get reminders before your job starts and when it's time to time out.
• My tools: see the tools lent to you and report a broken or lost one.
• File a leave, ask HR about a day in your DTR, and read HR's replies in your Inbox.
• Techy, the app's assistant, answers questions about tools, safety and company rules in English or Filipino.
• Sign in with your mobile number and your own PIN, or your fingerprint.

For Technocycle employees only: sign-in needs an account from HR.
